# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in short form. The full text is the normative version; the summary below is what we enforce here.

## The short version

**We pledge** to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

**We expect** empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions and experiences, and graceful
acceptance of constructive feedback. Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to those
affected, and learn from them. Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**We do not accept** sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing someone's private information
without explicit permission, or any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
a professional setting.

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and when representing
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions, comments, and commits that do not align with them, with a warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban, at their discretion and proportionate to the behaviour.

To report an incident, email **<conduct@example.invalid>**. Reports are reviewed promptly and
confidentially; the identity of the reporter is not disclosed without consent. Maintainers with a
conflict of interest recuse themselves. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

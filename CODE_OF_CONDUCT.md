# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/);
the full text is normative.

In short: **we pledge** to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation. **We expect** empathy, respect for differing
views, and graceful acceptance of feedback. **We do not accept** sexualized language or
attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, or publishing
someone's private information without permission. This applies in all project spaces —
issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report an incident privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3). Do not open a public issue about it. Reports stay
confidential: the reporter is not identified without consent, a maintainer with a conflict
of interest recuses themselves, and retaliation is itself a violation. Maintainers may
warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban, proportionate to the behaviour.
